Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence, or AI, is a field of computer science focused on creating systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.
AI system can perform tasks such as reasoning, larning, problem solving, understanding  language, recognizing images, and making decisions.
Commom applications of AI include virtual assistants, recommendation systems, autonomous vehicles, fraud detection, and medical image analysis.
machine learning is an important part of artificial intelligence because it allows computers to learn patterns from data.
