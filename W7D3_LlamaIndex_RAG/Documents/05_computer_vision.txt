Computer Vision

Computer Vision is a filed of Artificial Intelligence that enbles computers to understand and analyze images and videos.
Computer vision techniques are used  for image classification, object detection, face recognition, image segmentation, and medical image analysis.
Deep learning models, especially Convolutional Neutral networks and vision transformers, are widely used in computer vision applications.
Computer vision is used in auonomous vehicle, security systems, healthcare, manufacturing, agriculture, and many other industries.
