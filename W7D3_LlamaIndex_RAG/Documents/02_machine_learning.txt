Machine Learning

The majoe types of machine leanring are supervised learning, unsupervised learning, and reenforcement learning.
Supervised learnng uses labeled data for tasks such as classification and regression.
Unsupervised learning works with unlabeled data and can discover hidden patterns or groups.
