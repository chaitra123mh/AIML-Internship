Natural Language Processing

Natural Language Processing, or NLP, is a field of Artificial Intelligence that focuses on enlibng computers to understand, process, and generate human language.
NLP techniques are used for sentiment analysis, text classification, machine translation, question answering, chatbots, and information extraction.
Modern NLP systems often us tranformer-based models because they can capture relationship between words and sentence effectively.
Large language models are an important application of modern NLP and can generate human-like text based on the input provided by users.
