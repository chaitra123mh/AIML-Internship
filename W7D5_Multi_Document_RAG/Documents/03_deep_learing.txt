Deep Learning

Deep Learning is a subset of machine learning that uses neural networks with multiple layers to learn complex patterns from large amounts of data.
Deep learning models are particularly effective from image recognition, speech recognition, natural language processing, and generative AI.
Convolutional Neural Networks are commonly used fo image-related tasks, while recurrent and tranformer-based architectures can be used for sequence and language tasks.
Deep Learning usually requires significant computational resources and large datasets for training.
